# Pause

Balpreet says:

> word is pronounced Rahao and it means 'to pause'

## Method

Balpreet sent `Pause.jpeg` asked for it to be:

* 15cm tall
* Cut from a gold painted sheet of mDF

When cutting from painted material, it is best to cut from the back of the material, so that the paint is not burned.

Phil imported the picture into LightBurn and:

* Traced it to create a vector
* Resized the design to 150 mm tall
* Flipped the design horizontally, so that we can cut from the back of the MDF
* Rotate the design 45 degrees, so that it fits better on the MDF
* Centered the design on the laser's cutting area (390mm by 390mm)
* Configured the laser settings to cut through 3mm MDF:
    * Power: 100%
    * Speed: 500 mm/s
    * Passes: 7
* Saved it as `Pause.lbrn2`

Balpreet painted a 400mm by 400mm sheet of MDF gold.

Phil put it on a Mr Carve M3 laser cutter and cut the design.
